Our story

Built for personal style.

Velvet Vogue is an independent fashion concept created by John Finlo to make contemporary casualwear and formalwear feel accessible, expressive and easy to shop online.

What we believe

Good clothing should make everyday decisions easier. Our collections focus on clean silhouettes, wearable colours, versatile layering and pieces that can move from weekday plans to weekend moments.

We pair a calm visual identity with a simple online experience so customers can discover, compare and purchase without unnecessary friction.
